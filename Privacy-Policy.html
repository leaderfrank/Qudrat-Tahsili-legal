Privacy Policy

Itqan - Qudrat & Tahsili
Last Updated: July 30, 2026 App: Itqan - Qudrat & Tahsili Company: Koprey Country: Saudi Arabia

1. Introduction

Koprey ("we," "our," or "us") operates the Itqan - Qudrat & Tahsili mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information when you use the App.

The App is an educational question-bank and quiz platform designed to help students prepare for Saudi standardized exams (Qudrat and Tahsili). By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use immediately.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Account Information

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Email address — used for authentication and account identification.
  • Display name — a public name you choose that appears alongside questions you add.
  • Terms acceptance timestamp — recorded when you agree to our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy during registration.

Authentication is handled by Supabase Auth using email and password. No OAuth providers (Google, Apple, etc.) are offered. If your Supabase project requires email confirmation, you may need to verify your email before accessing the App.

2.2 User-Generated Content

You may voluntarily create and submit the following content:

  • Questions — including question text, multiple-choice answers, the correct answer index, source references, publication year/month, difficulty level, tags, and an optional written explanation.
  • Question images — optional images uploaded to illustrate a question. Images are compressed client-side to WebP format (max 800px, ~100KB target) before being uploaded to our storage provider.
  • Quiz attempts — records of quizzes you take, including your answers, time spent per question, and overall score.
  • Favorites — questions you bookmark for later review.
  • Reports — if you report an issue with a question, we collect the reason and an optional comment (max 500 characters).

2.3 Automatically Collected Information

The App itself does not automatically collect device identifiers, IP addresses, location data, or browsing history. However, our infrastructure provider (Supabase) may log standard connection metadata (such as IP addresses and request timestamps) as part of normal server operations.

2.4 Information We Do NOT Collect

The App does not collect:

  • Device identifiers (IMEI, IDFA, Android Advertising ID, etc.)
  • Precise or approximate location data
  • Contacts or address book
  • Photos or media files (except images you explicitly upload for questions)
  • Biometric data
  • Payment or financial information
  • Browsing history outside the App
  • Health or fitness data

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely for the following purposes:

  • To operate the App — create and manage user accounts, display questions and quiz results, and enable community features (adding questions, favoriting, reporting).
  • To improve the App — analyze aggregate usage patterns (e.g., popular categories, average quiz scores) to enhance the educational experience.
  • To moderate content — review reported questions and take appropriate action (admin moderation).
  • To communicate with you — only in relation to account-related matters (e.g., password reset) or material changes to our policies.
  • To protect security — enforce rate limits, prevent abuse, and maintain the integrity of the platform.

We do not use your data for:

  • Behavioral profiling or advertising targeting.
  • Selling or renting your personal information to third parties.
  • Automated decision-making that significantly affects you.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored and managed through Supabase, a backend-as-a-service platform. Supabase provides:

  • PostgreSQL database with Row-Level Security (RLS) — each user can only access data they are authorized to see.
  • Encryption in transit via HTTPS/TLS.
  • File storage for question images with authenticated uploads and public read access.
  • Authentication services with password hashing and session management.

While we implement industry-standard security measures, no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of your data.

5. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account remains active. You may delete your account at any time through the App's settings screen. Account deletion permanently removes:

  • Your profile and associated metadata.
  • Your questions (they are cascade-deleted along with their images).
  • Your quiz attempts, answers, favorites, and reports.

Some anonymized or aggregated data (e.g., statistical counts) may be retained after account deletion for analytical purposes. This data cannot be linked back to you.

6. Third-Party Services

The App relies on the following third-party service:

  • Supabase — provides database hosting, user authentication, file storage, and serverless functions. Your data is processed and stored on Supabase's infrastructure. Supabase Privacy Policy.

The App also uses the Tauri framework for cross-platform packaging. Tauri itself does not collect or transmit any user data.

No other third-party services (analytics, crash reporting, advertising, payment processing, or social media SDKs) are integrated into the App.

7. Google Play Store

The App is distributed through the Google Play Store. Google may collect information as described in Google's Privacy Policy. We have no control over Google's data collection practices.

The App uses the standard Material Design library for Android UI components. This library is part of the Android operating system and does not function as an analytics or tracking SDK.

8. Firebase

The App does not use Firebase or any Google Cloud Platform services directly. No Firebase SDKs (Analytics, Crashlytics, Authentication, Cloud Messaging, etc.) are included in the application.

9. Advertising

The App does not display any third-party advertisements. No advertising SDKs (AdMob, etc.) are integrated. We do not collect data for advertising purposes.

If this changes in the future, we will update this policy and notify users accordingly.

10. Permissions

On Android, the App requests the following permission:

  • INTERNET — required to communicate with the Supabase backend (load questions, sync quiz data, upload images, authenticate).

The App does not request access to:

  • Camera
  • Location (GPS)
  • Microphone
  • Storage / Media library (images are selected via the system file picker, which grants temporary access)
  • Bluetooth
  • Notifications

11. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will delete it promptly.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at the email address below.

12. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Update your display name or other profile information through the App's settings.
  • Deletion: Delete your account and all associated data through the App's settings, or by contacting us.
  • Data portability: Request your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Withdraw consent: At any time, for any data processing based on consent.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at the email address below. We will respond to your request within 30 days.

We take appropriate safeguards to ensure your data is protected in accordance with this policy regardless of where it is stored.
