1. Overview

Users of Itqan - Qudrat & Tahsili (the "App") may request the deletion of their account and associated personal data at any time. This page explains how to request account deletion, what data is deleted, and what data, if any, may be retained and for how long.

Your request is processed by Koprey (the App's developer) within a reasonable period after we verify that the request was made by the account holder.

2. Option 1 — Delete Through the App (Recommended)

Open the App and navigate to:

  1. Profile
  2. Settings
  3. Delete Account

You will be asked to confirm the deletion by entering your security code (a 4-digit code set in your account). Once confirmed, your account and associated data are deleted immediately.

3. Option 2 — Request by Email

If you cannot access your account (for example, because you forgot your password or no longer have access to the device), email us at:

koprey.app@gmail.com

To help us verify your identity, please include:

  • The email address associated with your account.
  • Your display name (if you remember it).

We will process your request within a reasonable period after we are able to verify that you are the account holder. Deletion requests sent from an email address that does not match the account cannot be processed.

4. Data Deleted

When your request is processed, we will delete:

  • Your account — including your login credentials and authentication records.
  • Your profile information — including your display name and related profile metadata.
  • Your submitted questions and other user-generated content associated with your account (unless required to be retained by law).
  • Your quiz attempts, answers, favorites, and reports — all activity records tied to your account.
  • Uploaded question images — images you uploaded are removed when the associated questions are deleted.

5. Data That May Be Retained

We may retain limited information if required:

  • To comply with legal obligations.
  • To resolve disputes.
  • To enforce our Terms of Service.
  • To investigate, detect, or respond to fraud, abuse, or security incidents.

Any retained data will be kept only for the period required by applicable law. After that period, it is deleted or irreversibly anonymized.

In addition, limited backups of our database may be retained for a short period for disaster-recovery purposes before being automatically deleted. Some anonymized or aggregated data (e.g., statistical counts) that can no longer be linked back to you may be retained for analytical purposes.

6. What Happens After Deletion

  • Your account will no longer be able to sign in.
  • Your profile and user-generated content will no longer be visible in the App.
  • Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. You will not be able to recover your account or its data by signing in again.
